
# InternTrack – Problem Discovery

## 1. Project Overview

**Product Name:** InternTrack

**Tagline:** One place to track every internship opportunity.

**Product Category:** Internship Application Management Platform

## 2. Problem Statement

College students often apply for internships through multiple platforms, including LinkedIn, Internshala and company career websites.

However, they lack a centralized system to manage their applications, deadlines, interview schedules and application updates.

As a result, students may forget deadlines, miss follow-ups and struggle to monitor their overall internship application progress.

## 3. Target Users

**Primary Users:** College students actively searching and applying for internships.

**Secondary Users:** College placement coordinators who need visibility into students' internship application progress.

## 4. Initial Problem Hypothesis

We believe students need a centralized platform that allows them to organize internship applications, track application statuses, manage deadlines and monitor their progress.

This hypothesis will be validated through user research.

## 5. Research Objectives

- Understand how students currently search for internships.
- Identify how they manage multiple applications.
- Discover their biggest application-tracking challenges.
- Understand their needs regarding reminders and interview tracking.
- Evaluate interest in a centralized internship management platform.

## 6. Research Method

- Primary research: Student surveys and interviews.
- Secondary research: Competitor analysis and market research.

**Note:** These are initial assumptions, not validated research findings.
